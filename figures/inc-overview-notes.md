# INC overview

Two panels compare the Direct baseline with early upload and ordered DONE.
The left side of panel B is a waiting-state snapshot; the right side shows the
later transmission order after READY and destination-write conditions hold.
Positions and lengths are not measured times. Only representative source ranks
and one destination are shown; this is not a physical topology diagram.

`inc-overview.drawio` contains native editable shapes, text, and connectors.
`inc-overview.svg` is the matching vector layout. The PDF was rendered from SVG
with `rsvg-convert` because the draw.io CLI is unavailable. No external icons are used.
The generated raster concept was used only as a composition reference, not as
the final paper figure. The manuscript includes `inc-overview.pdf`.
