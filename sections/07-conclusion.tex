\section{Conclusion}
H200 measurements show that pre-barriers remain near 15\us{} for cross-node EP8 Direct over the measured small-batch range and account for 15.2--16.7\% of sampled cross-node MoE decode time. \sys{} lets locally ready ranks upload while INC collects readiness signals, buffering early data until destination writes are safe. This changes the order of coordination and transmission without requiring receive-buffer addresses to be recomputed each round. The analysis relates the overlap to operation completion time and identifies staging capacity, readiness skew, and forwarding throughput as the main factors governing its benefit.
