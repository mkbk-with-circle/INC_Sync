\section{Conclusion}
Cross-node DeepEP V2 Direct uses a pre-barrier before payload injection and a post-barrier after sender-side completion work. H200 measurements show that pre-barrier remains near 15\us{} at EP8 across the measured small-batch points and occupies 15.2--16.7\% of the sampled cross-node MoE decode step. \sys{} proposes to overlap readiness aggregation with upload and to generate ordered completion notification at the network output. Its benefit depends on data visibility, safe buffer reuse, finite buffer capacity, and the delay exposed on the consumer's critical path. The protocol and model describe this opportunity; evaluating its net performance requires an implementation.
